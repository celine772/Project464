Free WIFI spots in Montreal

Find free Wi-Fi locations around Montréal and see which ones are near popular tourist attractions.

This map is for: Visitors to Montréal.

What you can do here: Black circles show Wi-Fi, blue circles show drinking fountains, and red circles show tourist attractions. Nearby points cluster together; cluster labels show counts such as 10+ and 20+. Click a cluster to zoom in, or click an individual point for details.

Map layers

The map displays Wi-Fi locations from internet.geojson, tourist attractions from tourist_attractions.geojson, and public fountains from Public_Fountains.csv. Fountain rows marked “accès restreint” have been excluded. Each layer has its own colored point clusters.

Where this comes from

  • Bornes du réseau sans fil MTLWiFi , Service de la planification stratégique, de la performance et de l'innovation, [licence], accessed 20/09/2026. I removed the columns that had inactive WIFI sites.
