About this map

This map helps visitors to Montréal find free public Wi-Fi, drinking fountains, and popular tourist attractions. It focuses on the City of Montréal and is designed as a practical guide while exploring.

Data sources & method

Wi-Fi locations are based on the City of Montréal’s MTLWiFi open dataset. The map also uses the project’s attraction and fountain datasets. Fountain records marked “accès restreint” are excluded; nearby points are grouped visually according to their screen position.

  • City of Montréal MTLWiFi dataset
  • Wi-Fi locations data
  • Tourist attractions data

Data and map presentation prepared for an academic project.

How to use this map

Marker colours identify the layer: dark markers are Wi-Fi, blue markers are fountains, and terracotta markers are attractions. Nearby locations cluster together; click a cluster to zoom in. Select an individual marker to read its details.

Search for a street address or place in Montréal. Choose a suggestion as you type, or submit the search to move the map to a match.
