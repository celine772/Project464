    About this map

    Visiting Montreal for the first time? Let this map be your guide to exploring the city with ease. Find free WI-FI spots to stay connected, public drinking fountains to keep hydrated, and explore the best attractions that the city has to offer.

    Tap the different symbols on the map to view more information on them, or use the dropdown menu in the corner of the map to filter locations and find exactly what you are looking for. Make the map your own, and take your stress out of discovering a new city.

    How to use the map

    Tap the different markers on the map to view more information on them, or use the dropdown menu in the corner of the map to filter locations and find exactly what you are looking for. Search for a street address or place in Montréal using the search bar. Choose a suggestion as you type, or press enter to move the map to a match. You can also save your favorite spots to the "Favorite Locations" tab at the top of the map. Simply click the star icon next to an address in the search bar to save it for later.
