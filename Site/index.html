    How to use the map

    Tap the different markers on the map to view more information on them, or press on the "Explore the layers" button in the corner of the map to filter locations and find exactly what you are looking for. Search for a street address or place in Montréal using the search bar. Choose a suggestion as you type, or press enter to move the map to a match. You can also save your favorite spots to the "Favorite Locations" tab at the bottom of the map. Simply click the star icon next to an address in the search bar to save it for later.

    Make the map your own, and take your stress out of discovering a new city.
