About

This map was created originally for the purpose of helping tourists and visitors find free WI-FI spots around the city in order to avoid paying for expensive roaming packages. However, it has since been expanded to include public drinking fountains and tourist attractions in order to provide a more comprehensive guide to exploring Montréal! I have developed this map around the idea of user-friendliness, and making it the easiest possible way for visitors to find the information they need. It can be very daunting visiting a new city and trying to find your way around, so I wanted to create a map that would take the stress out of explroing a new city. I hope that you enjoy using this map as much as I enjoyed creating it!
